===== SLIDE 7 — How? =====
To address this challenge, we developed xrd-app, a command-line computer vision
pipeline for automated processing of raw HDF5 nano-XRD data.
Pipeline: Raw -> Binning -> Peak Detection -> Contour Finding.  (CVEvolve)

===== SLIDE 8 — Binning =====
Sums NxN neighboring frames prior to detection. Reduces spatial grid size while
boosting per-position signal-to-noise ratio linearly with bin size.
Passes true coordinates (by interferometer), X and Y distribution, and snaps
each raw frame to its closest true coordinate on a square-pixel 1x1 grid.
Calculates the median of distances between points. Frames from the minimum X and
Y of the coordinates, making min(X,Y) an origin.
